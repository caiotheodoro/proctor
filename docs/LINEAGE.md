# Lineage

Proctor takes three instruments that already exist and points them at one question they do not ask.

**The floor.** Assay prices a detector against `flag_everything` under more than one cost profile, and publishes the interval. The floor here is the same idea: an arm that flags every priced class, so a schema check cannot win by being quiet. The profiles are `audit` and `enforce` because a missed wrong transfer and a blocked legitimate transfer are different losses.

**The oracle.** The forge line (reconciliation, binder-versus-policy, and the scaffolds around them) generates a known violation set and recomputes it with a program. The retraction that started that line is the reason H4 exists: four training seeds whose spread exceeded the headline margin. Proctor publishes the spread beside the margin, and refuses the margin until four seeds exist. Seed 999 is held out of training and out of selection.

**The other question.** A transport benchmark that holds the task fixed and varies how the model reaches the tool answers which pipe a small model survives on. Proctor holds the pipe fixed, because there is no pipe: the trace is already a list of calls. The question is whether the call was the right one. A schema check answers a slice of that (`schema_break`) and is silent on `wrong_unit`, which is schema-legal.

The author works on an AI gateway. `docs/ETHICS.md` is the containment. This repo does not call that gateway, and no hypothesis depends on it.
